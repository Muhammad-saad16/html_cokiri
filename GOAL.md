# Project Goal: City of Knowledge (IRI) Website

## 1. Purpose (Why)

City of Knowledge (Islamic Research Institute) is a non-political, non-profit educational and research institute based in Karachi, Pakistan. Its purpose is to propagate authentic Islamic teachings and produce positive social change through:

- **Ilm** (knowledge)
- **Da'wah** (communication / invitation)
- **Islah** (reformation)
- **Khidmah** (service)

The institute aims to foster intellectual growth, critical thinking, and scholarly dialogue within the Islamic community and beyond. This is achieved through structured courses, original research, publications, seminars, weekly study circles, and community outreach programs.

## 2. Target Audience (Who)

The website must serve the following audiences:

1. **Aspiring Scholars & Students**  
   Men and women seeking structured Islamic education, including the One-Year Shariah Diploma, Ilm-e-Din Course, Dars-e-Qur'an & Sirah sessions, and the 4-year Dars-e-Nizami (Aalim) program.

2. **Working Professionals**  
   Male professionals seeking part-time or evening programs, such as the 4-year Dars-e-Nizami (Aalim) degree designed for professionals.

3. **Local Karachi Community**  
   People attending weekly study circles, lectures, seminars, and institute events in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi.

4. **Online/Global Learners**  
   Individuals who want access to recorded lectures, webinars, publications, and online courses from anywhere in the world.

5. **Collaborators & Supporters**  
   Scholars, academic partners, community organizations, and potential donors or sponsors.

## 3. Value Proposition

> *Authentic, scholarly Islamic education and research rooted in tradition yet engaged with contemporary issues — led by credible academics and accessible to both on-site and online learners.*

Key differentiators:

- Non-political, non-profit credibility.
- Supervision by **Dr. Umair Mahmood Siddiqui**, a recognized Islamic scholar, former Member of the Council of Islamic Ideology Pakistan, and Assistant Professor at the University of Karachi.
- Blend of classical Islamic sciences and modern subjects such as philosophy, law, history, and contemporary issues.
- Inclusive learning opportunities for both men and women.
- Both physical campus programs in Karachi and online learning options.
- Active research library, scholarly publications, and public seminars.

## 4. Ideal Website Creation Approach

*(No specific technology stack is defined yet.)*

Build a modern, **English-only**, **mobile-first** website that acts as the central digital hub for the institute. The site should:

- Present the institute’s mission, vision, and leadership clearly.
- Showcase programs and courses with descriptions, eligibility, duration, and schedule.
- Provide an admissions or inquiry process for prospective students.
- Display an events listing or calendar for seminars, lectures, and study circles.
- Host or link to publications, research, and media content.
- Integrate the institute’s existing social media presence (Facebook page, YouTube channel).
- Include contact details, a location/map, and clear calls to action.
- Be fast, accessible, and easy to maintain.

**Important:** Content is managed through Sanity Studio (`/studio`), so the client's team can update programs, events, gallery, testimonials, videos, books, and site text themselves without a developer. Schema/structure changes (new content types, new fields) remain a developer responsibility.
